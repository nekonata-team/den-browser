# Provide an Interactive Tutorial Board

An empty Desk cannot teach Den's primary operations, especially Den Mode, through use alone. Provide at most one app-owned Tutorial Board per Den, launchable from an empty Desk or with `:tutorial` in Open Board; pair Den-owned lesson content with a resumable checklist advanced by semantic actions inside Den. The ongoing content maintenance is justified by helping users understand the product before they leave.

## Consequences

Use Den's own tutorial material instead of relying on third-party demo sites. The checklist advances on outcomes such as opening a Board or entering Den Mode and never records arbitrary keystrokes or typed content. Reopening either entry point resumes the existing Tutorial Board.
