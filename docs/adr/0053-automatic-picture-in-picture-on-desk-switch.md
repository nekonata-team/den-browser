# Automatically enter Picture in Picture when switching Desks

Den Browser optionally enters native Picture in Picture when switching Desks. When enabled, the live Web Board that was the Focused Board in the outgoing Desk is asked to move its currently playing HTML5 video into Picture in Picture; paused videos, Terminal Boards, and Boards without a live runtime are ignored. The setting is app-wide and disabled by default, and returning to the Desk does not automatically exit Picture in Picture because WebKit entry is best-effort and the user may keep the floating video while working elsewhere.
